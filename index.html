AVIDRIVE OFFICIAL POLICY

Privacy Policy for Avidrive

Effective Date: September 3, 2026

Avidrive ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your personal privacy and maintaining total transparency. This Privacy Policy outlines how Avidrive handles your information when you use our Google Drive media viewer application.

1. Zero Server Storage & Architecture

Avidrive is engineered with a strictly client-to-cloud architecture. We operate zero proxy servers, zero analytics trackers, and zero external databases. When you use Avidrive, your mobile device establishes a direct, encrypted connection directly with Google's official servers.

2. Information We Access & How It Is Used

  • Google Account Identity: When you sign in with Google, we access your name, email address, and profile picture solely to verify your account session inside the app.
  • Google Drive Files (drive.readonly): Avidrive requests read-only permission (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.readonly) to allow you to stream and view your personal photos and videos stored in Google Drive.
  • Zero Storage Bloat: Avidrive does not save your photos or videos into hidden persistent device caches. All streaming happens in volatile memory on-demand.

3. Google API Limited Use Disclosure

Avidrive's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not transfer, sell, or disclose your Google Drive data to any third parties or use it for personalized advertising.

4. Data Security & Encryption

All communication between Avidrive and Google Drive APIs is encrypted in transit using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS). In addition, the app applies Android FLAG_SECURE to protect your screen from unauthorized capture by third-party background software.

5. Your Rights & Data Revocation

You have full control over your data at all times. You can disconnect Avidrive and instantly revoke all access to your Google account at any time by visiting your Google Account security dashboard: Google Account Permissions.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions, feedback, or privacy inquiries regarding Avidrive, please contact the developer via your Play Store contact email.
